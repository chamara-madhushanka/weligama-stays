Frequently Asked Questions

How do I meet my driver at the airport?

Your driver will wait at the main exit gate inside BIA Colombo arrivals holding a sign board with your name clearly written on it.

What if my flight is delayed?

We monitor flight schedules live! Even if your flight is delayed, your driver will be waiting for you with zero additional waiting fees.

Are expressway / highway tolls included?

Yes, all expressway tolls, parking fees, and driver charges are included in our quoted fixed prices.

How and when do I pay?

You pay cash directly to your driver after safely arriving at your destination. We accept USD, EUR, GBP, or LKR.
